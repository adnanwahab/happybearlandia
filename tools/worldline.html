    How this works: each future draws 9 assumptions from the ranges above, then simulates 2,000 sampled households (each standing for ~100 real ones) choosing where to work and whether to drive or take transit. Road delay comes from a congestion curve on 9 corridors, resolved over several rounds so drivers react to each other. Home prices respond to demand versus supply. The baseline and the change share each future's random draws, so the difference between them is the effect of the change, not noise. The city, coefficients and costs are invented for the prototype and are not calibrated to any real place.
