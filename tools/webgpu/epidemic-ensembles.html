Synthetic population: 2,000 people, initially 10 infected. Each lane samples transmission within ±15% of the chosen rate and evolves an SIR model with daily normal-approximated binomial transitions, rounded and bounded to conserve population. Teal band: middle 80%; white: median; faint lines: sampled outbreaks. Illustrative model, not a calibrated health forecast.

How to interpret this experiment

Compare presets, then change one rate at a time. A larger transmission-to-recovery ratio generally supports growth; random events still produce different outcomes. The shaded interval describes variation between simulated outbreaks, not confidence in a real-world forecast. Identical parameters and seed reproduce the same experiment.
