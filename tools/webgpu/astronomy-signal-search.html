What am I looking for?

A tall peak reveals a repeating signal hidden in random noise. Increase noise to make the search harder; increase trial frequencies to locate the peak more precisely. The amber marker shows the signal you injected.

Choose an observation preset, then run the scan to compare the injected and recovered frequencies.

Model & parallel workload

The GPU subtracts the mean and computes sine/cosine matched-filter power for every trial frequency across 2,048 samples. This direct Fourier scan is intentionally small and inspectable, not an optimized FFT or an analysis of real astronomy data.

Seeded, reproducible random inputs. GPU compute generates the results; Canvas 2D draws the chart after readback. WebGPU dispatch reference.
