How to read the 25-hour goal

EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIMS
01

Endurance is one continuous run.

The main bar measures the longest uninterrupted span supported by heartbeats in one session. Gaps are visible, and restarts do not add together.

02

Being online is not coding.

Read coding, publishing and waiting separately. An idle runner can stay online for 25 hours without delivering 25 hours of coding. Worker-active time is not proof of useful work.

03

Output still needs review.

Follow task outcomes and new draft PRs alongside the time. A zero exit code, a merged readiness report or a PR count alone does not prove tests or acceptance passed.

Times are measured from recorded events, not estimates. No time after the latest recorded event is added to the totals. External assistance that the runner cannot observe may require separate review.
