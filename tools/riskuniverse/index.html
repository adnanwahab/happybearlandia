Model, definitions & limits

Three correlated geometric Brownian-motion assets, buy-and-hold, no leverage or rebalancing, 12 steps over the chosen horizon. Per step: S ← S × exp((μ − σ²/2)Δt + σ√Δt Z). Each asset shares a common Gaussian factor with loading √ρ and an independent factor with loading √(1−ρ). Every scenario has its own deterministic hash stream, transformed with Box–Muller. Model version RU-GBM-1.

Loss = starting capital − terminal value. VaR is the empirical confidence quantile of loss, and expected shortfall is the mean of the worst ceil((1−confidence)×N) losses. These signed measures may be negative when the entire selected tail is profitable. Fixed-seed reproducibility is intended within the same browser/GPU; floating-point math can vary across devices.

This lab models synthetic portfolios only. It excludes jumps, fat tails, liquidity, defaults, fees, tax, and changing correlations. Insurance claims and company cash-flow models are not implemented. Results describe the chosen assumptions and are not calibrated forecasts.
