## Mechanism and provenance

Reuses buildPedestrianNetwork and its sidewalks.v1 offsets/trimming. Six stations
on a single lane never exceed 12m between stations. Select nearest endpoint within
80m; no plausible-looking invented coordinates or fallback route. Entry cuts to
the selected lane, with five subsequent hops. Height/look-ahead and clearance are
presentation assumptions documented beside the code. Loaded roads retain their
existing synthetic/mapped source; no geographic observation is claimed.

Camera and target alone change. No resident command, identity mapping, RNG, model
clock, simulation rule or outcome changes; ODD inventory needs no model revision.
Compared executable pedestrian geometry and camera collision contracts against
renderer documentation. No new social mechanism. Stats instrument CPU updates and
frame intervals during active transitions, not GPU performance. Listener and DOM
cleanup is bound to pagehide. No background loop.
