Population scoreHousing computes employed-member mean commute from district distance at an assumed 25 km/h; it is not measured travel time. Its capacity test is district housingUnits × multiplier against represented households. Current district stays are available by construction. householdDecisions additionally imposes savings and utility-gain gates, on its existing monthly stagger within the daily clock. A shortlist reusing only scoreHousing would omit those move gates.

Monthly City housing uses resolved available inventory after dependencies, applies rent/bedroom filters, reserves each unit/household at most once, and records HOUSEHOLD_RELOCATED inputs/outputs plus decisions/moves. Unmatched households legitimately remain without a unit. Its accessibility contribution is a ranking term, not a commute eligibility constraint. Adding a hard commute rule is a model change requiring version/replay/ODD review; it cannot be described as already supported.
