## Next integrated slice and proposed contract

Prerequisite owner must expose a read-only selected City run/arm/month in World
with an explicit mapping to the displayed World revision and geometry. Treasury
and project anchors must be supplied with evidence and coordinate-system identity;
the City fixture has no observed treasury location. Unsupported mappings stay
unlocated. No producer/model rule or retention changes are authorized by this
proposal alone.

Proposed adapter fields (not an implemented API): schema version, run and scenario
IDs, model/rules/dataset versions, initial-state digest, seed, revision, month and
time unit, currency, completeness, expenditure scope (capital or capital plus
operations), authoritative total and source reference. Entries carry stable
source identity, amount, expenditure category, policy/project IDs when supported,
evidence references, and a verified anchor mapping or explicit unlocated reason.
Use the same versioned snapshot for chart, trails and text. Distinguish an
explicit complete zero ledger from absent/partial/invalid evidence.

Reconcile all included expenditure entries against the authoritative monthly
system total before animation. Reject negative/nonfinite amounts, duplicate source
identities, wrong arm/run/month, mixed currencies and unexplained discrepancies.
Any numerical tolerance must be declared and tested, never hidden by rounding.
For display precision, use a documented rounding/allocation policy so visible
line amounts sum to the visible selected total. Exclude revenue, balances, debt,
planned costs and unspent allocations. Never synthesize a balancing line item
unless the source contract actually identifies that residual expenditure.

Missing locations retain their full amounts in a static unlocated-cost chart and
accessible list. Missing totals or incomplete evidence report unavailable rather
than zero or a reconciled total. Missing project identity/anchors prevent trails,
even if district or a later project is known. Changing the selected run/month
must clear stale amounts, anchors and effects together.

Once prerequisites exist, implement one bounded read-only World selector/view:
decorative coins only between verified mapped endpoints; static chart for all
unlocated costs; keyboard/touch inspection and complete text equivalent. Label
paths as decorative accounting links, never physical money movement or evidence
of causal project outcomes. Coin count/speed must not imply transactions, people
or probabilities. Suppress animation for reduced motion, hidden views and fallback
renderers; dispose resources on selection change, close and route exit.

Acceptance: committed deterministic fixture from the real producer plus positive,
zero, partial/missing, invalid, duplicate, mixed-run and unlocated cases; exact
reconciliation including display rounding; immutable source state and seeded
replay; rapid month/arm changes; focused desktop/mobile browser captures through
`/world`, keyboard and reduced motion. Measure frame-time distribution and
geometry/material/listener/frame-loop disposal on a disclosed consumer device at
the visual cap, or explicitly retain hardware acceptance as unavailable.
