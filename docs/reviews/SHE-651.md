## Delivered behavior

World controls → Tape measure mounts in the existing Three.js `/world` renderer.
Picks use visible loaded building shells, or the existing y=0 scene ground plane
within camera far distance. Tags preserve building source IDs and kinds. Snap
checks base/top source polygon corners within two world metres; disable it for
free surface picking. Measurements show Euclidean distance, absolute vertical
difference and horizontal span. Footprint is the projected source polygon area of
the second building, not the area implied by two points. No surveyed-dimension
claim is made. Ground has no footprint. Standalone Canvas2D does not mount this
renderer/tool; residents, vehicles, trees and ornaments are not measurement targets.

Touch/pointer picks reject drags; focus scene and Enter picks center. Reset is a
native button; Escape clears points and returns focus. Native controls have 44px
minimum heights and explicit focus outlines. The overlay is static in every motion
preference. Geometry/material disposal occurs on reset/replacement/pagehide;
listeners are removed on pagehide. No extra animation loop or per-frame raycast.
`window.hblTapeMeasure.getStats()` exposes pick timing/failures and frame counts,
frames over 33.34ms and maximum frame interval while measuring or displaying a
tape. These are local diagnostics, not hardware performance acceptance evidence.
