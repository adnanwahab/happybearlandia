## Finding and checked contracts

Read repository AGENTS.md, README.md, docs/design.md, affected implementation-plan
sections, world/hidden-systems/README.md, world/city/README.md,
world/counterfactual/README.md, docs/models/README.md and docs/models/city.md.
No docs/architecture.md exists.

- `world/hidden-systems/model.js` runs Monthly City from one synthetic initializer
  with matched seeds. It projects permits, construction, projects and one
  household. `page.js` and `index.html` expose that capacity comparison, not
  supply-chain edge selection. Construction dependencies cannot stand in for
  business-to-business dependencies.
- `world/city/systems.js` businesses pack closes firms quarterly after six bad
  months. Demand uses district household income, foot traffic, recession, flood
  and utilities; costs use rent, operations, wages and utilities. BUSINESS_CLOSED
  identifies a business and viability. There is no supplier edge or shipment
  event linking one business closure to another.
- `tools/flowmap/logistics.js` executes synthetic network deliveries;
  `business-connector.js` requires explicit demand IDs and converts volume and
  hours to external supply fraction and delay days. This is usable infrastructure,
  not a verified City-business mapping.
- `world/models/business-v1/external-adapter.js` emits account observations and
  contributes edges, but its snapshot has `events: []`. The underlying
  `index.js` reports bankruptcy, separately from City's closure mechanism.
  Supplier reliability also uses seeded draws; that draw alone cannot evidence
  a selected network-edge cascade.
- `world/counterfactual/economic-domains.js` supports logistics.link-closure
  with exact boolean closed and explicit checkpoint mappings. It passes deliveries
  to Business and employment to House; it does not produce closure event chains.
  Its README correctly states that private authenticated storage is a deployment
  prerequisite and the default handler fails closed. Do not enable it for this
  puzzle or equate its business periods with City months.

Material decision: do not relabel a financial observation, random supplier failure
or construction delay as an executed supply-chain closure event. The prerequisite
is a producer-owned closure/cascade contract, not additional decorative rendering.
No model rules changed, so the existing City ODD remains accurate.
