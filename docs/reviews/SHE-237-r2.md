## Evidence and replay contract shared by every situation

**Authority.** P means the existing Population/House authority and minute clock; C means monthly City; I means the recorded-contact idea overlay. Never identify C residents with P residents by display name, array index or district. Cross-authority stories require a versioned mapping of IDs, calendars, units and semantics first. A C monthly social tie is a synthetic same-district proxy, not evidence of a P face-to-face encounter.

**Readiness.** “Reuse” means the core mechanism exists, with story detection/persistent explanation still proposed. “Extend” means at least one causal mechanism is proposed and must be implemented and documented before displaying the claim. Neither label certifies a finished story journey. Every discovery surface described below is a proposed addition to the named existing entry point unless explicitly identified as existing.

**Common record R.** Each candidate retains model/rules/source revision, optional subsystem versions, data sources, synthetic assumptions, seed, normalized parameters, full starting state/checkpoint digest, immutable parent, run/branch IDs, calendar origin, time units, fixed step partition, ordered commands/interventions and shocks. Preserve exact entity IDs, ordered transition IDs, pre/post values with units, decision alternatives/reasons, and causal input/output references. Each narrative clause points to those records. Inputs, modeled observations, matched differences and interpretation occupy separate fields. Authored fixture edits are declared initial conditions, never hidden player actions.

**Replay R.** Open the saved pre-trigger checkpoint, play recorded commands at their original committed times under the pinned implementation and step partition, and compare intermediate and terminal authoritative state digests to retained evidence. Also restore midway and continue. Show the dependency chain edge by edge; a timestamp alone supplies no causal edge. Where source records lack a dependency, retain the decision inputs or add explicit provenance in future work; until then display an observed sequence, not a proved cause. A changed intervention creates a child branch, not an edit to history. Compare with a control from the same full starting state and matched random streams; validate actor identity before person-level comparisons. Config changes affecting generation need separately disclosed initial states, not a claim of identical checkpoint forks.

**Retention and failure.** Population `contracts.js` retains bounded events (`maxLogEntries`, default 400), 12 traces/entity and at most 1,024 traced entities. Hotspots retain 120 encounters; traditions retain 12 gatherings/192 invitations. Archive cited evidence before eviction under a proposed bounded, versioned report contract; current checkpoints do not recover discarded history. Preserve Population `1.3.0` / `population-checkpoint.v1` compatibility and its 64 MiB cap. Missing checkpoint, old rules, absent actor, truncated evidence, rejected action and no qualifying change are explicit outcomes. Rendering and opening an inspector must not advance time or RNG. Narrative text never writes state or invents dialogue, motives, purchases, diagnoses, eviction or friendship.

Thresholds below are proposed editorial detection rules, not model coefficients or measured incidence rates. Show all qualifying and missing outcomes in comparisons; select an example only with its seed-selection policy disclosed. One matched pair illustrates that model realization, not a population effect estimate.

## The 25 situations

### 1. The employer closes, and tomorrow's routine disappears

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. District demand, staffing-limited revenue, costs and cash produce closure; actual employer assignments are removed. A bakery-specific customer-routing cascade would be an extension: P firms currently use aggregate district budgets. See `population/economy.js` and `employment.js`.
- **Trigger:** computed firm cash crosses below zero at daily settlement while at least one resident is assigned there.
- **Record:** R plus revenue, payroll, tax, recruitment/rent costs, closing cash, `BUSINESS_CLOSED`, employer/headcount changes, `JOB_LOST`, next work interval and actual earned/settled pay. Preserve accrued wages and other household resources.
- **Discovery:** resident employment inspector shows the lost assignment, with a firm-to-household timeline; a map headline opens those exact records. Do not say every neighbor changed shops.
- **Replay:** R through the cash boundary → assignment removal → next scheduled work → settlement. A funded synthetic control tests the cash assumption; prohibit automatic reassignment in the explanation unless recruitment actually occurs.

### 2. A new park bench becomes a shared memory

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Hotspot appeal competes with distance, seeded preference and schedules; travel must finish before proximity-gated social interaction. `population/hotspots.js` supplies existing improvements and encounter records.
- **Trigger:** an accepted benches improvement is followed by a recorded encounter between actual stationary visitors.
- **Record:** R plus place ID/improvements, destination choices separately from arrivals, visitor IDs/intervals, encounter pair and before/after canonical ties.
- **Discovery:** existing hotspots panel links “these residents met here” to their profiles and the dated encounter; show “no encounter” when none qualifies.
- **Replay:** R from before improvement through choice, travel and co-presence. Compare an unchanged park; free improvements imply no construction cost or budget benefit.

### 3. Dinner leaves a trace after the plates are cleared

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Household ingredients debit savings, elapsed cooking prepares servings, actual dining changes fullness; recent personal meal experience can affect later conversation response. `house/model.js` and `house/relationships.js` share canonical residents and cash.
- **Trigger:** completed cooking, attended dinner, then an eligible completed conversation within its supported recent-experience window.
- **Record:** R plus ingredient transaction, servings, preparation/dining person-minutes, attendees, fullness, meal memory and conversation factors/tie deltas. A tie increase is conditional, not promised.
- **Discovery:** House needs/payments/profile panels expose a meal → experience → later response receipt for the actual participants.
- **Replay:** R across preparation, attendance and conversation. Compare the same conversation without the meal; restore mid-cook to check one charge and no early servings. No meal memory may be attributed to an absent diner.

### 4. An apology changes one relationship without erasing its history

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Completed argument and apology choices alter directed trust/resentment and shared tension/memories under `house/relationships.js`.
- **Trigger:** two available residents finish an argument and subsequently finish an apology; detect a recorded decrease in resentment or tension.
- **Record:** R plus admission, both participants' completed minutes, action IDs, directed before/after values and both memories. Keep unresolved tension visible.
- **Discovery:** House relationship profiles show two perspectives on the same completed actions, not invented speech or forgiveness.
- **Replay:** R from pre-argument through both completions; equal-duration ordinary-chat control separates background changes. Duplicate completion after restore must have no second effect.

### 5. The same sick day has two financial endings

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Employment leave entitlements change paid absence; actual work/leave ledgers settle household wages and represented employer payroll once.
- **Trigger:** the same authored absence overlaps a scheduled shift in branches differing only in paid sick leave entitlement.
- **Record:** R plus illness-spell assumption, schedule, entitlement balances, worked/paid/unpaid minutes, accrued/settled wages, employer payroll and household savings/outflows, with raw versus represented units.
- **Discovery:** employment inspector opens a paired household payment receipt: same modeled absence, different pay. No health improvement claim.
- **Replay:** R over absence and settlement, then from a mid-pay-queue checkpoint. Declare any config-dependent initialization difference. The intervention is a synthetic policy/config experiment until normal policy admission is supplied.

### 6. Friday music develops a circle of regulars

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Authored recurring traditions use preferences, invitations and prior attendance in destination choice; travel, weather and schedules gate actual attendance.
- **Trigger:** the same resident attends two recorded Friday music occurrences, optionally following stage support.
- **Record:** R plus occurrence/calendar/place IDs, support, invitations and statuses, actual attendees/person-minutes, returning IDs and encounters separately.
- **Discovery:** existing traditions panel opens the two attendance records and resident links; regular attendance alone is not friendship.
- **Replay:** R across both calendar-aligned occurrences; retain records before eviction. Compare support on/off with matching schedules; missed invitations remain visible. The event template itself was authored, not culturally invented.

### 7. A friend invites someone who never arrives

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Existing friendship-based tradition invitations coexist with ordinary obligations, travel and energy constraints.
- **Trigger:** a recorded invitation becomes missed at its occurrence end while another invited pair attends.
- **Record:** R plus invitation sender/recipient, event bounds, accepted activity decisions, travel/attendance intervals and final status. Record a specific blocking reason only when the decision evidence establishes it.
- **Discovery:** traditions invitation list contrasts invitation with actual attendance; a resident link reveals their recorded activity instead of “they felt rejected.”
- **Replay:** R through invitation and event end; a controlled schedule change may test one constraint. Missing reasons stay unknown, and rejection/missed status creates no invented resentment.

### 8. Childcare opens a parent's working day

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Care capacity, affordability and attended care constrain a guardian's work availability and once-only household outflow (`population/children.js`, employment/behavior).
- **Trigger:** an eligible child gains an available affordable care place and the designated guardian records less care-related work absence.
- **Record:** R plus child/guardian/household mapping, eligibility, seats, fees/subsidy exposure, attendance, guardian absence/work minutes and actual wages/payments.
- **Discovery:** linked child and employment inspectors show care attendance alongside the guardian's shift; label academic exposure as a proxy.
- **Replay:** R across enrollment, care and shift with capacity-only control; verify one care charge and no double wage credit. Subsidy exposure is not a government spending ledger.

### 9. Two households share a rent shock but not a buffer

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Actual daily rent/income/spending settlements deplete savings at household-specific rates; existing risk analysis requires reconciled full-day windows.
- **Trigger:** after a disclosed rent configuration change, one household has a supported risk transition while another does not.
- **Record:** R plus persistent household membership, initial savings, income/rent/care/spending ledger, seven full days for runway and fourteen for trend, missing/stale flags.
- **Discovery:** existing household risk lens opens both payment histories and matched household IDs across arms; describe shrinking resources, not eviction.
- **Replay:** R for the whole observation window, holding each household's identity fixed. Compare treatment/control within each household before comparing them; unequal initial buffers are recorded assumptions.

### 10. A vacancy exists, but the business cannot afford to hire

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Job slots, recruitment budget, seeded job search and caregiver availability jointly gate employer assignment.
- **Trigger:** an unemployed eligible resident remains unmatched with no funded vacancy, then obtains an actual assignment in a funded comparison.
- **Record:** R plus candidate eligibility, vacancy/cash, recruitment cost, draw/rule reason, employer headcount, hire event and first actual shift/pay.
- **Discovery:** employment inspector explains the funded match or unavailable match; an opening is not a job offer and hiring is not immediate wages.
- **Replay:** R through daily recruitment and next work interval; compare declared initial cash only. Check budget/headcount conservation and zero pay before work.

### 11. A shorter working week leaves a shop understaffed

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Workweek/off-day rules change actual staffing; revenue depends on productive staffing while contract pay may be retained.
- **Trigger:** a four-day schedule produces a recorded staffing gap relative to the same firm's baseline.
- **Record:** R plus worker schedules, off-days, actual work/payroll, staffing ratio, demand, revenue and cash; follow downstream closure only if it occurs.
- **Discovery:** firm's employment-linked timeline shows who was off and the modeled staffing constraint; it must not infer poorer service from animation.
- **Replay:** R across one full comparable week using identical demand inputs; distinguish schedule treatment from employee turnover. A no-gap result is valid.

### 12. The commute disappears, but work still happens

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Eligible remote-role schedules replace commuting with actual home work; room/workspace conditions can affect supported productivity.
- **Trigger:** a remote-work configuration changes a resident's committed travel/work intervals relative to their baseline.
- **Record:** R plus role eligibility, home/employer IDs, schedule, travel minutes, work minutes, productivity/pay factors and any paid workspace installation.
- **Discovery:** resident activity ledger beside employment and House workspace panels; call it time saved only from recorded matched travel differences.
- **Replay:** R across the same shift with eligible remote setting changed; no unsupported universal remote entitlement. Preserve installation completion and one debit if furniture is included.

### 13. A witness sees what an absent partner cannot

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Consenting eligible romantic conversation can trigger witnessed-jealousy changes only for an actual nearby adult dating partner.
- **Trigger:** a completed eligible interaction has a qualifying observer; absent/traveling/other-location controls must not qualify.
- **Record:** R plus explicit consent/adult gates, dating ties, participant attendance, observer position/location/travel state, directed deltas and witness memory.
- **Discovery:** observer's relationship memory links the actual witnessed event; no rumor or omniscient knowledge is added.
- **Replay:** R before conversation completion. Any arranged observer location is an authored starting condition unless reached by a real visit. Never infer consent from a seeded crush or romantic score.

### 14. Friends drift apart without a dramatic fight

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse. Time-based relationship drift and expiring sentiments coexist with completed interactions (`house/relationships.js`).
- **Trigger:** a recorded relationship loses strength or a temporary sentiment expires across a no-contact interval.
- **Record:** R plus last interaction, drift update boundaries, tie values, sentiment expiry and completed contacts. Incomplete retained history cannot prove “no contact.”
- **Discovery:** profile shows dated interactions and the explicit drift rule, leaving a gap label if earlier contacts were evicted.
- **Replay:** R through the exact elapsed interval and a contact-preserving control; updates must apply once when either participant is inspected/advanced. Do not narrate deliberate avoidance.

### 15. A transit change creates an unexpected acquaintance

- **Mechanisms:** P / Extend. Reuse transport mode/destination choice and real hotspot encounters. Add a supported spatial transit intervention that changes arrival opportunities; do not borrow C travel metrics as P encounters.
- **Trigger:** treatment changes a resident's actual arrival window and a new co-present encounter qualifies.
- **Record:** R plus route/service input, travel alternatives and chosen components, arrivals, venue intervals, encounter/tie transition and intervention-to-travel dependencies.
- **Discovery:** route timeline opens the shared place and both profiles, calling it an encounter until recorded friendship supports stronger language.
- **Replay:** R with/without one service change; show travel → co-presence → encounter, not transit → friendship by timestamp. No encounter remains an honest outcome.

### 16. A storm brings strangers into a mutual-aid network

- **Mechanisms:** P / Extend. Weather can gate existing activities, but shelter admission, aid transfers and durable aid ties require explicit capacity/resource/interaction rules on P. Storm grid/C hazard values need a verified spatial/time adapter first.
- **Trigger:** a recorded hazard closes access and eligible displaced residents use the same admitted shelter or aid queue.
- **Record:** R plus hazard source/cell/time, exposure, closure, shelter capacity/occupancy, actual transfers, co-presence and consequent ties; distinguish unmet requests from delivered aid.
- **Discovery:** shelter map marker opens actual assistance receipts and participants, without invented heroism.
- **Replay:** R through exposure → access loss → admission → transfer → interaction; matched weather baseline and capacity sensitivity. Reject unmapped cells and conserve supplies. Co-location alone is not mutual aid.

### 17. Six homes become someone's first stable address

- **Mechanisms:** C / Reuse. Existing construction gates create units, then affordability/bedroom/ranked allocation rules may house an unhoused household. Reuse SHE-49 small-infill recipe; “stable” requires an explicitly observed duration.
- **Trigger:** a completed project is followed by an actual qualifying household allocation that remains housed through the stated window.
- **Record:** R plus project/building/unit IDs, funding/permits/resources, completion event, applicant alternatives, allocation/lease, burden and residence duration.
- **Discovery:** Future Machine recorded building/outcome timeline links the household and its monthly housing record. P interiors need a separate mapping.
- **Replay:** R month by month from project proposal through allocation, with no-project control. Completed units do not guarantee a particular beneficiary; never substitute the requested six for completed supply.

### 18. A delay divides two otherwise matched futures

- **Mechanisms:** C / Reuse. Governance delay shifts the earliest project progress; normal finance, construction and housing constraints determine subsequent outcomes. Reuse SHE-49 delayed-housing example as a starting fixture.
- **Trigger:** matched branches differing only in delay yield different recorded completion or household housing dates.
- **Record:** R plus delay schedule, gate reasons, capital/capacity, completion month and same-household monthly assignments/burden.
- **Discovery:** paired Future Machine timeline follows one C household at identical months; label absence/departure explicitly.
- **Replay:** R with a single delay difference from one initial state. SHE-49's three examples vary multiple inputs, so comparing their catalog outputs alone does not isolate delay causality.

### 19. A small zoning change alters a child's opportunities

- **Mechanisms:** C/P / Extend. Reuse C parcel/development/housing constraints and P child attendance/exposure rules only after explicit household/child/location/calendar mapping. Add residential-move-to-school access rules; long-term attainment is unavailable.
- **Trigger:** supported zoning permits actual completed housing, a household actually moves, and a mapped child has a changed recorded school-access/attendance window.
- **Record:** R plus zoning/versioned parcel, project/lease/move, identity mapping, school/care capacity, route and attended exposure minutes. No future salary or college outcome without a model.
- **Discovery:** parcel-to-household-to-school evidence chain describes changed modeled exposure, not a predetermined future.
- **Replay:** R through each gate in both clocks with the mapping pinned. Stop at the last supported edge if move or mapping is absent; a zoning approval is not a completed home.

### 20. Drainage protects homes but leaves less capital elsewhere

- **Mechanisms:** C / Reuse for flood-resilience and housing alternatives; Extend for a claimed joint budget-allocation trade-off. Existing `city/policy-comparison.js` supplies bounded synthetic housing/drainage alternatives, not hydraulic flows.
- **Trigger:** a recorded flood produces different damaged housing in drainage/control arms; a housing delay attributed to spending additionally requires an explicit binding common budget decision.
- **Record:** R plus policy costs/finance, resilience, shock severity/duration, event-time damaged units, repairs, housing project funding/gates and household assignments.
- **Discovery:** policy comparison exposes damage at the flood month and later recovery separately; a capital ledger supports any “instead of housing” claim.
- **Replay:** R with identical shock timing and one policy difference. Inspect damage when it happens, not only final repaired stocks. Never equate the resilience proxy with water depth or prove opportunity cost by comparing unrelated runs.

### 21. Two applicants compete for the same affordable home

- **Mechanisms:** C / Reuse. Eligibility, bedroom/affordability constraints and seeded conflict resolution allocate a scarce unit atomically.
- **Trigger:** at least two eligible households rank one available unit and exactly one receives it.
- **Record:** R plus unit inventory, both applicants' constraints/ranks, allocation rule/draw keys, winning lease, losing alternatives and later assignments.
- **Discovery:** household inspector opens a shared allocation decision with both outcomes; avoid “deserving” or discriminatory motives unsupported by rules.
- **Replay:** R from pre-allocation month with exact applicant order/rules; same inputs reproduce the winner and inventory conservation. A larger-supply control tests scarcity without editing the recorded allocation.

### 22. New homes wait behind a utility connection

- **Mechanisms:** C / Reuse. Construction requires capacity and other resources; funding a utility constraint can enable progress, but households benefit only after completion/allocation.
- **Trigger:** a retained utility-capacity gate blocks a funded project, then a supported capacity change permits the next stage.
- **Record:** R plus capacity/demand units, project reservations, other blocking gates, upgrade admission/spend, progress/completion and downstream allocations.
- **Discovery:** project-unblock inspector shows the exact gate and affected building/households, leaving unfilled units visible.
- **Replay:** R before the binding gate and after upgrade, using no-upgrade control. Verify the capacity gate actually clears and another constraint has not become binding; spending alone is no completion proof.

### 23. One encounter carries an idea across neighborhoods

- **Mechanisms:** I / Reuse. Existing idea diffusion reads retained P contacts; adoption/remix/fading are seeded overlay rules, not new movement or behavior in P.
- **Trigger:** a retained cross-neighborhood contact transmits an active idea and a later contact carries it farther.
- **Record:** R plus immutable source save, exact contact projection/order/IDs, starter/title, recipe version, overlay seed, adoption/remix/decline/expiry events and source contact evidence.
- **Discovery:** existing idea explorer links each transmission to the saved contact and original resident identity; a “bridge” label needs both contact edges.
- **Replay:** rebuild I from the same bounded projection and recipe, tracing contact → draw → adoption → subsequent contact. It must not mutate the source save or invent omitted contacts. Adoption is not endorsement or a policy change.

### 24. A market's absence reveals who depended on it

- **Mechanisms:** P / Reuse for disabling an authored tradition and observing attendance/destination/encounter changes. Extend for lost vendor income: current tradition/food-stall records do not provide an individual purchase ledger.
- **Trigger:** disabling the Saturday market changes recorded attendance or a resident's chosen activity compared with an enabled control.
- **Record:** R plus enabled setting, occurrence/support/venue, actual activity decisions, travel, attendance and encounter deltas; separately retain any future sales/stock/payroll ledger before financial claims.
- **Discovery:** traditions panel follows former attendees' actual Saturday activities, including people whose routine did not change.
- **Replay:** R over the same calendar interval from before disabling; preserve obligations/weather. An invitation not sent is not loneliness, and absent market attendance is not lost business revenue.

### 25. A neighborhood loses its everyday bakery

- **Mechanisms:** P / Extend. Combine actual business closure with explicit business-to-place mapping, shop availability in destination choice, household purchases/substitutes and repeat co-presence. Existing authored Riverside Bakery `bakery-v1` is a bounded guided fixture, not proof of this general Population cascade.
- **Trigger:** computed closure removes a mapped bakery from feasible shopping and at least one resident actually chooses/reaches a substitute.
- **Record:** R plus firm/place mapping, closure ledger, shop hours/availability, actual purchase/stock/price/budget records, rejected alternatives, changed trips and encounters. Only recorded changes justify neighborhood-wide scope.
- **Discovery:** closed storefront opens a before/after routine map with affected and unaffected households, then their receipts and meeting places.
- **Replay:** R through cash → closure → availability → choice → trip → purchase/encounter; matched funded control and supply conservation. Until those links exist, use item 1's honest employer story or the labeled authored bakery fixture.
