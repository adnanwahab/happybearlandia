## Finding

The requested authentic, source-linked history cannot currently be attached to
storefronts through the designated entry point without inventing an identity or
location mapping. No executable change is proposed on that basis. Missing history
continues to produce no placards. This is not acceptance of the successful path.

Read repository AGENTS.md, README.md, docs/design.md, the implementation plan,
world/city/README.md and docs/models/README.md. No docs/architecture.md exists.
The README architecture explicitly separates City, Population and housing clocks.

Paths compared:

- `world/business.html`, `world/business-lab.js`: entry opens Entrepreneur Mode,
  a form-based company simulator, with navigation to neighborhood mode. It is not
  a walkable storefront view. Saved company definition transfer explicitly calls
  the neighborhood a separate visitor simulation.
- `world/models/business-v1/index.js`: period/seed/definition-version financial
  reports, bounded logs (48) and history (120); no sourced establishment-history
  records, verified owner, calendar opening date or storefront identity. Bankruptcy
  is a modeled financial condition, not evidence of a historical shop closure.
- `world/business-mode.js`: session placement uses a World building index; toggle
  changes an open boolean, without a retained opening/closure event or source ID.
- `world/sim.js`: visitor purchases create text events with tick and local building
  index; newest 60 retained. These records lack stable event IDs and do not form
  sourced establishment history. A purchase cannot support an opening story.
- `world/city/seed.js`, `world/city/systems.js` businesses pack: quarterly synthetic
  BUSINESS_OPENED/BUSINESS_CLOSED events do exist. Generated openings have business
  and district IDs but no building assignment. District placement cannot establish
  a particular shop window. No owner or historical calendar date is provided.
- `world/city/engine.js`: event IDs are month/ordinal scoped to a run; preserve
  experiment/scenario/run scope and model/rule provenance before exposing links.
  A bare event ID is not globally unique. City months do not map to World ticks.

No model rules, inputs, clocks, retention or identity contracts changed; existing
ODD descriptions remain applicable. No new ABM or empirical claim is introduced.

## Next integrated slice

1. Establish a producer-owned, versioned history projection on one authority.
   Include source/run identity, stable event identity, supported event type,
   business identity, exact model time and evidence classification. Preserve
   missing owners/dates. Changes require explicit before/after source records.
2. Establish a verified business-to-building mapping and same-run/time selection
   at the walkable entry point. Unmapped district events remain unavailable for
   shop windows. A district history panel would be a separate product decision.
3. Add a bounded read-only projection and source inspector, with no narration
   inferred from status snapshots. Hide absent/unsupported histories; failures
   must clear stale cards and expose a truthful unavailable state.
4. Test source identity collisions across runs, missing mappings, unsupported
   types, malformed/failing reads, deterministic replay and rapid selection.
   Verify the normal entry journey at mobile sizes, keyboard focus and touch;
   provide a static reduced-motion presentation. Instrument local frame deltas
   and listener/RAF/DOM cleanup; do not enable live analytics.

Prerequisite: a reviewable producer contract and mapping, plus an explicit choice
of walkable World integration versus Business Lab history presentation. No external
service or fabricated fixture can supply that mapping. Real historical stories
would additionally require sourced editorial records and verified site identities.
Acceptance requires successful and unavailable/failure browser journeys after that
contract exists; a schema-only utility would not fulfill the player experience.
