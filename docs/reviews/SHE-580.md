## Delivered behavior and contracts

See ../../world/future-machine/ui/README.md for retention, trust boundaries and limits.
The normal page mounts native buttons, labeled invitation field, polite status
and reaction fieldset. A viewer matches the canonical Month 0 checksum locally
before following. The service has no City storage/model execution dependency;
state is limited to validated month and landing context. Host-only updates,
independent control, latest-snapshot reconnect, expiry and termination do not
mutate run history. Reactions are explicitly cosmetic and capped at eight.
Requests are body-bounded, room capacity bounded, same-origin checked and no-store.
Room lifetime is absolute, not renewed by polling. No personal identifiers are
collected. Bearer invitation holders may view/react; possession grants access.

This is sampled presentation synchronization (500 ms plus network delay), not a
hard real-time guarantee. Full camera quaternion bookmarks and renderer conversion
are outside the existing landing contract; incompatible camera states stay local
with an explicit message. It does not synchronize model computation or silently
load/recompute a run on behalf of a remote host. Page reload requires manual rejoin.
