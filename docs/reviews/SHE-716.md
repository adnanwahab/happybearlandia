## Delivered behavior and decisions

World controls links to `/world/coalition/`. The pinned authored transit decision
has three supporters, a renter opponent and a service abstainer, derived through
the existing council evaluator. Cards drag between trays or use keyboard/touch
buttons, keeping focus after moves. Only three actual supporters in the selected
bloc win; duplicates, opponents and abstentions cannot inflate that count. No
card movement changes a vote. Cancel clears selection and restores launch focus.

All roles, numbers, seed/month identifiers and input checkpoint are explicitly
synthetic authored toy data, separate from observed measurements, City engine
outputs and scripted explanation. Both arms retain the same seed/month. There
are no random draws, mutable parent checkpoints, persistence, policy commits,
network calls or analytics. This does not invent a City run or infer real-person
preferences. Current World state is not used as this exercise's data source.

Reopening verifies the exact pinned source and recomputed expected positions.
No source, cancellation or incompatibility yields a winning result. This is
source/rule drift detection, not cryptographic integrity or engine replay proof.
Arbitrary source imports and recorded City-run adapters are intentionally outside
v1; their identity/time and independently retained replay evidence are prerequisites
for later integration. No live service/editorial/hardware prerequisite is required
for the toy itself.
