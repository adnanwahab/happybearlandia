## Delivered behavior and contract

Selection renders Household Cost Atlas in the existing replay panel, with
experiment/run/scenario/seed/model/rules identity, final-horizon cohort burden and
three reconciled public spending categories. Each category opens the exact
aggregate run JSON with its source field named. All spending categories are
included; unsupported private costs, financing incidence and household bills are
explicit exclusions. Counts and eligibility are unavailable, not zero. Missing,
invalid, inconsistent and unsupported-version evidence produces unavailable bins.
There is no residual allocation or claim to know who actually finances policy.

See `world/decision/COST-ATLAS.md` for field/units/version, missing-data,
reconciliation tolerance, source and lifecycle contracts. Representative fixture
executes the producer with seed42, 2years, 600m annual voucher funding and 3bn
annual construction subsidy, all synthetic. Other defaults are versioned.
The atlas uses a final recorded metric, not a new causal calculation. No model,
RNG, resident-state, clock or rule changes; docs/models/README.md's aggregate
housing boundary and housing README remain accurate. No new ODD model required.
