## Plan and acceptance

Own a separate read-only journey-trail adapter and focused tests; minimal hooks
in Population world-view. Capture only the selected resident's received committed
positions, scoped to runtime ID/revision, model version, departure and endpoints.
Keep at most 32 samples / 30 model minutes; never generate prehistory. Missing
positions or skipped ticks break the ribbon. Changing trip/run, seeking backwards,
closing details or deactivating clears the transient history. Static scene geometry
and native buttons support reduced motion, touch and keyboard inspection without
moving the resident or clock. No new model, RNG, run IDs, seeds or outcome fields.

Prerequisite: supported current-trip snapshots from Population; other clocks and
legacy preview remain unsupported. These are synthetic modeled residents, not
observations of real people. Visual connections between adjacent received samples
are presentation only, not evidence of an observed street route.
