## Delivered behavior and decisions

world/render/magnetic-picking.js ranks frontmost host-provided hits sampled in
CSS pixels, deduplicates identity keys, and exposes near ties explicitly.
Maximum 49 rays, 24-pixel radius, 3-pixel tie tolerance. These are authored UX
parameters, not measurements. Hidden ancestor/material hits are excluded.
The host orders people, buildings, journals and vehicles by depth at each ray;
magnetic picks retain source IDs and route through the existing inspectors.
The native modal has 44px minimum choice heights, focus restoration and
Tab/Enter/Escape support. Scene Enter/Space picks near its center. Preview is
static, pointer-transparent and throttled to 10 Hz; reduced motion needs no
animated fallback. Chooser activation rechecks visibility and active mode.

This bounded sampling approximates silhouettes; it can miss thin entities
between rays. It does not prove globally nearest visible geometry. Scene-wide
occlusion beyond existing selectable geometry is not newly certified.
Living Population, Business mode and standalone Canvas2D use existing pickers.
Low-end runtime cost and screen-reader behavior need device/browser verification.
