## Results and measurement boundaries

| Requested result | Six-hour result | Required evidence and denominator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified unique visitors | unknown | Approved uniqueness unit, consent/eligibility and bot/internal exclusions; deduplicated qualifying entries in a confirmed UTC [start,end) window. Session IDs are not people. Report excluded and unobserved populations separately. |
| Source reach | unknown | Timestamped, bounded per-channel exports with source/version and coverage. Keep impressions, clicks and visitors distinct; overlapping platform reach cannot be summed as unique people. Hidden referrer means unknown source. |
| Activation | unknown | Accepted meaningful first interaction / eligible entered sessions, raw numerator, denominator, missing count and cutoff. Page load is not activation; deduplicate transitions within the same schema/session. |
| Sharing | unknown | Settled copy/native/download outcomes, each with its eligible denominator. Separate intent, success, cancellation, failure, referral landing and friend activation. Copy success cannot establish delivery or friend play. |
| p95 first-play performance | unknown | Same-build route/backend/device/network sample set and precise start/end milestone; nearest-rank p95 over sorted valid durations, with sample count, failures, timeouts and missing starts reported separately. No averaging of per-channel p95s. |
| Incidents | unknown | Bounded incident ledger plus explicit coverage statement; severity, UTC start/end, impact, cause confidence, mitigation and recovery evidence. No supplied ledger does not mean zero incidents. |
| Acquisition cost | unknown | Verified incremental channel spend, currency, window and attribution scope / qualified acquired visitors; also show spend / activated acquired sessions separately. Unknown or zero denominator produces unknown. Labor and hosting allocation require separate definitions. |

First-play schema v1/v2 keeps its explicit City version pairing; never relabel
old receipts as current. It has relative elapsed time and ephemeral page IDs,
so it cannot independently place entries into a wall-clock cohort or deduplicate
people across visits. Local journey counters also cannot supply this scoreboard.
Use prerequisite owners' reviewed aggregates; this report adds no collector.
