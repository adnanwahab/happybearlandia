## Next integrated slice and proposed contract

Prerequisite: a model-owned retained vehicle-passage producer, with explicit
road mapping and coverage. Generating a new fleet/routing mechanism would change
model scope and requires a separately specified authority and ODD review.
Decorative car loops, person totals and district flows cannot substitute for it.
No external feed, live analytics or production service was enabled.

Proposed read contract (design only, not an implemented or accepted schema):

- Pin schema, run, source, model/rules and scenario versions, seed (or explicit
  nonrandom status), authority, clock units and half-open `[start,end)` interval.
- Supply stable road-link IDs with geometry revision, direction and explicit
  mapping to source way/node segments. Never join by array index or nearby shape.
- Retain unique passage evidence IDs, trip and vehicle references, link ID and
  crossing timestamp. Specify crossing semantics and duplicate suppression;
  repeated legitimate crossings remain separate passages. Vehicle passages,
  unique vehicles, trips, passengers and represented population are separate units.
- Expose per-link coverage as complete, partial or unsupported, including gaps,
  dropped records and retention limits. An absent row is unavailable. Zero is
  valid only for a fully covered link/interval with no recorded crossings.
- Aggregate from those exact records and expose their evidence references to
  selection. Sample count means included vehicle passages; separately show trip
  count if deduplicated and supported. Retain the same immutable run in all views.

Once this exists, mount one bounded overlay through `/world` World controls.
Use road-surface brightness for supported counts, an explicitly labeled
**Traffic pressure** legend, sample count and interval with time units. A proposed
normalization is `count / max(1, maximum covered link count)` for the selected
interval, mapped monotonically to brightness. Display the denominator; it is
relative sampled usage, not probability, road damage, capacity utilization or
cross-run comparability. Freeze a common denominator for comparisons or disclose
that they cannot be compared. Zero stays distinct from missing purple/hatching;
partial coverage must remain separately labeled and excluded from full-coverage
normalization. Selection opens the corresponding actual trip aggregate and
coverage, not an illustration cabin or fabricated journey.

Acceptance work, in dependency order:

1. Producer fixture and invariant tests: deterministic pinned synthetic records,
   positive/zero/missing/partial links, duplicate evidence, legitimate repeated
   crossings, direction, exact interval boundaries and retention gaps. Assert
   aggregate sums reconcile to included evidence and input records are unchanged.
2. Adapter tests: geometry/source/run mismatches, malformed values, read failure,
   changing intervals and runs, stale selection, all-zero counts and missing
   normalization. No clock advancement, RNG consumption or source mutation.
3. Normal `/world` browser/visual check: open control, enable/disable overlay,
   inspect bright/zero/missing links, verify aggregate/source identity, keyboard
   and touch selection, visible focus, reduced motion and unsupported renderer
   messaging. A standalone fixture preview cannot establish normal-entry wiring.
4. Bound geometry/update work and measure frame time and GPU/resource/listener
   disposal across repeated toggles, run changes and page exit on a disclosed
   consumer device. Report unavailable hardware explicitly.

Observed sources here are OSM geography where supplied. Synthetic assumptions
belong to their City/Population authorities. Computed pressure would be a
read-only aggregate of a declared future passage source. Current decorative
traffic, grain and wet-road shading carry no usage or condition evidence.
No new social/agent mechanism, rule, source identity, seed or clock was introduced;
the ODD inventory therefore remains unchanged.
