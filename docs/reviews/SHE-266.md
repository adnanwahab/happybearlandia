## Delivered source and contracts

- `world/future-machine/data/families.js`: deterministic versioned seed/City-ID
  identity; exact unique household → reciprocal occupied unit → building join;
  maximum absolute matched-month rent-burden ranking, missing pairs last and
  stable ID tie break. No simulation RNG consumption or record mutation.
- `world/future-machine/ui/family-card.js` and `ui/styles.css`: synthetic names
  and procedural faces, paired WITHOUT PLAN/WITH PLAN cards, rent/income meters,
  home icons and moved/stayed badges, and a monthly bar sparkline. Every numeric
  card link opens exact current-month records with run/seed/model/arm; selecting
  a bar opens that specific month's records. A subsequent card link restores the
  current record rather than retaining an earlier bar's record.
- `ui/index.js` replaces the visible legacy paragraph panel in the normal
  `createUI` entry with the family card. The lower-level panel interface remains
  intact for existing consumers. `ui/household-timeline.js` gives the secondary
  timeline picker the same seeded names and ranking.
- `main.js` passes the same completed history to the card and wires
  `onFollowFamily` to the current stage. Scene `index.js` resolves each arm on
  every shared frame. Both Three and Canvas2D highlight their own recorded
  buildings without changing camera, clock, simulation or saved history.
- `ui/README.md` documents semantics and missing states. The ODD inventory is
  unchanged: these are presentation identities, not new agents or behavior rules.

Occupations are not invented from seeds; this model does not supply the requested
bakery-worker biography. Stayed/moved compares the selected unit to Month 0;
it does not assert there were no intervening moves. Bar height clips at 100%,
while accessible labels and linked raw records retain exact values. Missing
households, occupied units, positive income or building mappings remain explicit.
