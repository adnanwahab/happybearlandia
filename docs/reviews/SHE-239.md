## Seven competing missions (all hypotheses)

### M1 — Visual wow factor: make the difference legible

Challenge: does more animated city detail actually improve memory of the intervention? Existing split/blink views, schematic buildings, outcome chart and construction cues already communicate change.

- **A — Difference spotlight:** restrained noncolor outline and exact-record label at the first physical divergence; reuse `scene/divergence.js` and `diff/first-divergence.js`. Low effort hypothesis; risk of exaggerating a small difference.
- **B — Construction sequence:** animate recorded project stages and progress, with reduced-motion still frames; reuse `scene/construction-progress.js` and `construction-ghosts.js`. Medium effort; risk of implying continuous state between monthly observations.
- **C — Evidence-first composition:** prioritize synchronized chart, compact building inventory and one static paired scene; reuse `ui/outcome-timeline.js` and scene mappings. Medium effort; risk of less spectacle but potentially clearer recall.

Decisive experiment: baseline and three prototypes show identical Months 0/15/16/120, plus a no-change pair. After 60 seconds, ask visitors to identify what physically changed, when and whether it is a real parcel. Proposed eligibility: at least 10/12 correct on each factual question and no invented inventory in source checks. Choose the eligible candidate with most correct unaided change recall after five minutes, requiring at least two more correct participants than baseline; otherwise retain baseline. Tie: lower measured frame cost. A memorable false parcel claim disqualifies the design. Recheck heatmap semantics from SHE-161 only on its own authority; no automatic City overlay.

### M2 — Scientific credibility: show what the result warrants

Challenge: a deterministic single seed can look like a forecast or uncertainty estimate.

- **A — Assumption receipt:** one compact expandable input/version/units/limitation receipt on existing KPI cards; reuse `ui/provenance.js`, `evidence.js`. Low effort; disclosure may be ignored.
- **B — Sensitivity comparison:** bounded paired seed sweep and supported housing amount/delay alternatives, displaying distributions and missing runs; reuse recipe/data interfaces. Higher effort; seeds alone do not quantify structural uncertainty and a sweep UI/orchestrator needs proof.
- **C — Claim challenge:** make visitors classify one result as observation, synthetic assumption, modeled result or interpretation before revealing the evidence; reuse existing explanatory UI. Medium effort; quiz friction may discourage exploration.

Decisive experiment: ask visitors to interpret rent, cumulative capital spending, one shared-seed pair and a missing measurement using the same results. Proposed gate: at least 10/12 distinguish a model result from a forecast and identify absent uncertainty; all exported units/inputs agree with history. B additionally requires matched-start replay, recorded seeds, failures and a clearly labeled empirical spread rather than a confidence interval. Choose highest four-question accuracy with at least two more correct complete responses than baseline; tie: less time to answer. If none improves, retain baseline. Never rank by producing a preferred policy outcome. Any model-rule change requires its own ODD/code review and versions.

### M3 — Emergent human stories: make one consequence matter

Challenge: does an aggregate housing headline tell a more memorable story than a traceable household change? Narrative must not invent agent motivations.

- **A — Follow one family:** retain the existing synthetic family card and reciprocal home highlights through the paired timeline; prioritize a user-selected City household. Low effort; selection bias and missing occupancy remain visible.
- **B — Three turning points:** short editorial captions anchored to exact household records at three months; reuse `story/index.js`, `story/moment-narrative.js` and household timeline. Medium effort; captions may imply causation.
- **C — Distribution before protagonist:** show who changes most, least and not at all in matched rent/income burden, then let the visitor choose; reuse `data/families.js` ranking. Medium effort; extreme selection can exaggerate typicality.

Decisive experiment: same pair, same eligible household set; after five minutes ask visitors to recount one supported change and reopen its exact arm/month/record. Include missing household and nonreciprocal home fixtures. Proposed gate: 10/12 successful exact-record reopening, zero fabricated actions/motives in generated captions, and synthetic naming visible. Winner needs at least two more accurate story-and-source recollections than baseline; tie: fewer clicks. If the history contains no qualifying household change, report insufficient material rather than author an event. No House/Population identity link without an adapter.

### M4 — Causal explainability: answer WHY without overclaiming

Challenge: can a dependency diagram teach more than a precise evidence chain when traced causal records are absent?

- **A — Guided evidence ladder:** readable policy/project/completion/building/unit steps beside raw receipts; reuse `story/evidence-trail.js` and its view. Low effort; association may be mistaken for causal proof.
- **B — Controlled rerun comparison:** retain seed/start and remove or delay only the housing proposal, explaining the resulting model contrast. Medium effort; requires new run receipts and does not prove real-world causality.
- **C — Explanation boundary panel:** offer supported answers and explicit unknowns, with exact sources and no graph; reuse `evidence.js` and trail unavailable reasons. Low effort; may feel less exploratory.

Decisive experiment: identify why the building appears at Month 16, then remove the completion event, policy reference or selected snapshot in separate synthetic fixture probes. Proposed gate: 100% missing-link probes disclose the gap; 10/12 visitors correctly distinguish recorded relationship, controlled model comparison and empirical causation. Winner improves exact evidence retrieval by two participants over baseline without increasing unsupported causal answers; tie: lower retrieval time. B must also preserve original hashes and show a zero-treatment control. Any candidate asserting that an event link alone proves causation is eliminated.

### M5 — Interactivity and gameplay: give change a purpose

Challenge: are more policy sliders more engaging than a bounded question with inspectable consequences?

- **A — Discovery challenge:** reuse `ui/discovery-quest.js` to find the first physical change and inspect it. Low effort; success may become a mechanical click sequence.
- **B — Budgeted housing choice:** ask visitors to choose among supported amount/cost/delay recipes under a declared illustrative capital cap, then compare actual outputs. Medium effort; cost is a declared input, not an estimated construction price. Reuse existing recipe/policy controls and SHE-131 constraints only where units and authority are demonstrably compatible.
- **C — Predict then inspect:** record a session-local prediction for homes and completion month, run the ordinary pair and reveal error plus source. Medium effort; avoid rewarding accidental guesses or preferred policy direction.

Decisive experiment: ten-minute normal-entry sessions, with null intervention, impossible target and failed rerun cases. Proposed gate: no unsupported policy execution, stale result or reward from automatic replay; at least 10/12 complete a deliberate edit → run → inspect loop. Winner needs two more self-initiated second experiments than baseline while factual accuracy does not fall; tie: fewer required steps. Impossible goals must state infeasibility, not alter the model. Treat district allocation and flagship integration as distinct until mapped.

### M6 — Architectural performance: spend resources on useful work

Challenge: does adding GPU work improve this bounded demo more than reducing replay transfer or main-thread work?

- **A — Compact replay transport:** reduce duplicated serialized state while preserving normalized history and evidence. Higher effort; versioned API compatibility and reconstruction integrity are prerequisites.
- **B — Bounded worker normalization:** move canonicalization/diff preparation off the main thread with cancellation and stale-result guards; reuse existing worker conventions. Medium effort; transfer/copy costs may erase gains.
- **C — Presentation resource reuse:** retain sorted data, scene resources and update-on-change behavior across scrub operations; reuse `scene/resources.js` and `demo-performance.js`. Medium effort; risk of stale geometry or disposal leaks.

Decisive experiment: benchmark baseline/A/B/C on 30 and 120 starting households, fixed seed 42 and identical 120-month histories through the normal page/API. Preregister timings after a baseline measurement. Proposed selection gate: identical full normalized state/event/metric digests and selected evidence after reverse scrub, canceled rerun and reopen; no resource growth across ten replace/dispose cycles. Winner must reduce its declared primary bottleneck (ready latency, interaction p95 or bytes) by at least 20%, with no more than 5% regression in other measured timings; tie: smaller engineering cost. If variation exceeds the effect, inconclusive. Honor SHE-11 → SHE-40 budgets → SHE-44 device validation; no million-agent claim without model/oracle and separate benchmark evidence.

### M7 — First-run simplicity and reliability: deliver the first useful action

Challenge: is renderer quality or immediate trustworthy inspectability the best first impression?

- **A — Inspectable Month 0 first:** prioritize existing initial model preview, then allow an explicit fork while rendering initializes; reuse `main.js` preview and stale-request guards. Low effort; preview must not look like a completed future.
- **B — Guided preset tour:** make the existing tour the first visible action, with pause, manual takeover and deterministic retry. Medium effort; automated motion may hide control or delay usefulness.
- **C — Conservative first renderer:** offer a clearly labeled fast Canvas/inventory entry followed by optional graphics enhancement; reuse backend/fallback contracts. Medium effort; physical low-end hardware benefit remains unmeasured.

Decisive experiment: fresh-storage homepage → Play Demo → one supported edit, run, exact-record inspection and share/reopen. Inject renderer-init timeout, WebGL failure, missing saved run, invalid receipt, network failure and late superseded success. Require explicit failures, working retry/cancel, preserved completed comparison and truthful backend/provenance. Proposed gate: every automated fault case passes and at least 10/12 visitors complete the normal journey unaided. Winner needs two fewer abandoned sessions than baseline; tie: measured time to first successful meaningful action. Strict receipt links must never silently create a substitute run. SHE-169 recovery is reused, not a claim of GPU-compute restoration. No low-end or cross-browser acceptance without actual device evidence.

## Six-dimension scoring rubric

Score only eligible candidates after experiments. Each dimension is 0–5, higher is better; cost and risk are reverse scored. Weighted total is `sum(weight * score / 5)`, maximum 100. Record evidence and uncertainty for every cell; unknown scores remain null and totals are provisional rather than treating null as zero.

| Dimension | Weight | Anchors (0 / 3 / 5) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User impact | 30 | No useful task completed or worse baseline / proposed mission gate met with observed benefit / clear preregistered improvement in task success and recall, replicated beyond initial sample |
| Novelty | 10 | Repackages existing behavior / materially different useful interaction / previously unsupported insight with tested advantage; spectacle alone earns no credit |
| Verifiable functionality | 25 | Contradicts records or no executable proof / normal-entry success plus relevant failure probes / independent reproducibility and full preregistered matrix, including missing evidence |
| Reuse of existing infrastructure | 15 | Duplicates model/authority / uses documented contracts with a bounded adapter / composes existing normal entry points without duplicate state or widened contracts |
| Engineering cost | 10 | More than five engineer-days or unbounded dependency / two–three days including tests/docs/integration / at most half a day verified end to end |
| Risk | 10 | Unresolved integrity/auth/identity failure / bounded known risk with tested mitigation and rollback / no new state authority, migration or external dependency; complete applicable failure evidence |

For intermediate scores use explicit interpolation: 1 substantial deficit, 2 partial, 4 exceeds the 3 anchor but falls short of 5. Cost score 4 means over half a day through one day; 2 means over three through four days; 1 means over four through five days. Estimates include integration, accessibility, tests, documentation and review; actual effort replaces estimates after execution. Risk scoring must name scientific, UX, compatibility and operational risks, not a generic confidence adjective.

Hard gates: exact provenance and immutable parent; compatible IDs/time/units; no invented outcome; supported normal-entry functionality; applicable failure-path evidence. A failed gate means reject; a blocked gate means unranked. No weighted score rescues either. Mission thresholds above are proposed decision rules, not measured product performance budgets. Freeze any justified revision before running and retain its rationale.

Within a mission, apply its decisive experiment first, then use the rubric for tradeoffs among eligible candidates. Retain baseline if improvement is not demonstrated. Across missions, compare winners using the common rubric, dependencies and a proposed three-engineer-day integration envelope; choose a compatible portfolio, not necessarily all seven. Prefer the Pareto frontier of impact, correctness and cost. Scores within three points are a tie: prefer verifiable functionality, then lower cost/risk; if still tied, run the smallest discriminating experiment or defer. The director records rejected alternatives and uncertainty. No winners or invented scores are declared in this specification.
