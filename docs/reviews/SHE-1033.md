## Model and contract review

Compared `docs/models/README.md`, all seven ODD sections in `docs/models/city.md`, the integration contract, schema/fixture and `world/policy-preview-{api,contract,security}.js` against Constitution City `runtime-core.js`, `plan.js`, `mappings.js`, `analysis.js`, `views.js` and City `seed.js`, `contracts.js`, `engine.js`. No new ABM or rule changes. Existing monthly City remains authoritative. Frozen initial state, seed derivation, model/runtime/mapping versions, source IDs and exact legal hash are retained. Legal review is asserted by the authorized caller. Illustrative $1000/mechanism is an assumption; source text does not infer funding or beneficial outcomes. Three synthetic households, 24 months and 100 pairs are bounded demonstrations, not a national forecast or ten-year model.

Checkpoint implementation deterministically recomputes selected months while producing the job, verifies their metrics against full-run monthly observations, persists those states, and later reads them without browser simulation. Do not describe this as independently recorded full-state checkpoint equivalence: metric compatibility is checked, not a second full-state reference hash. Post-policy month zero contains authorization changes. Events retain latest 96 per arm, with omissions disclosed; incomplete graph attribution remains incomplete. These limits agree with the integration description; no model-description change is needed.

Response pagination is still needed for practical integration performance: viewer currently fetches all results during polling, potentially repeating ~25 MB transfers. `runIndex` helps one selected pair but there is no lightweight distribution summary or cursor/page response. Measure slow/mobile memory and latency before claiming the 100-pair embedded journey is ready. This is documented follow-on work, not a reason to fork the contract.
