## Evidence checked

- Read root AGENTS.md, README.md, relevant product-design and implementation-plan
  content, model inventory, Population ODD, and review-directory guidance.
  `docs/architecture.md` is absent.
- `world/business.html` loads `world/business-lab.js`; it is a Business Lab ledger
  and trace player, not the World camera renderer.
- `world/models/business-v1/index.js`: `simulatePeriod` computes aggregate
  `operatingHours = production.hours * laborFraction`. Its customer trace has
  synthetic `tick`, `minute`, customer, segment and purchase reason. Day traces
  use 480 ticks with `minute = 540 + tick`; month traces use one tick. Neither
  reports opening/closing events nor specifies a business calendar or timezone.
- `world/business-lab.js`, `startPeriod`, `renderRun`, `updateRun`: the whole
  period is resolved before playback. Display progress is advanced by a timer;
  displayed 09:00–17:00 is an authored playback convention. Trace selection uses
  customer count times progress rather than exact trace minute. The initial
  “Doors are opening…” narrative is not an authoritative opening event.
- `world/models/business-v1/external-adapter.js`: connected observations retain
  context, entity identity and synthetic provenance, but its snapshot emits
  `events: []`. The operating-hours observation supplies a duration, not an
  interval. Zero staffing is not evidence for a real shop closure.
- `world/business-mode.js` and `world/sim.js`: legacy World uses a player-owned
  `business.open` toggle and a generic 07:00–21:00 daytime gate. This is a
  separate authority without a verified Business Lab identity/time mapping.
- `world/population/economy.js`: `open` denotes synthetic firm viability;
  BUSINESS_CLOSED / BUSINESS_OPENED concern cash exhaustion / new capital,
  not daily business hours. Employee shifts in `world/population/employment.js`
  do not establish shop opening hours. Population ODD describes daily firm
  updates and committed worker intervals; it does not establish a Business Lab
  scheduling bridge. No rules or ODD descriptions were modified.

## Required next slice

run/model/rules version, source record or event ID, authority-scoped business ID,
clock units/epoch, supported interval bounds and explicit missing-data semantics.
Choose the actual source; do not import Population or legacy clocks by matching
local IDs. Confirm whether this remains the Business Lab route or a World
renderer integration. These are repository data/interface prerequisites, not a
requirement to enable external services or supply production credentials.

Once that source exists, project it read-only into cosmetic sign, shutter and
light states. Missing, invalid, mismatched or out-of-coverage records must show
neutral/unavailable status. Specify exact boundary and event ordering semantics
in the source contract, then test them in the projection. Label synthetic
schedule facts and cosmetic geometry separately. Do not infer opening from a
purchase, an employee shift, profitability or the animation progress clock.

   route actually owns those controls; no stale visual state or duplicate props.
4. Keyboard and mobile status access, reduced-motion static cues, low-end asset
   limits, route teardown and cancellation of timers/listeners/owned resources.
5. Before/after evidence on the affected route with disposable local fixtures.
