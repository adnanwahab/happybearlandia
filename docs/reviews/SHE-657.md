## Next end-to-end slice and acceptance plan

Prerequisite: a reviewed local model-owned trajectory producer/read contract.
It must retain immutable run, model/rules version, seed and scenario provenance;
vehicle and trip IDs; clock units and ordered timestamped supplied positions;
coverage intervals and explicit gaps; confirmed departure/arrival and destination
where actually modeled. Specify retention limits, invalid-data behavior and any
cross-authority mapping. This is proposed work, not an implemented schema.
An external transit feed or production configuration cannot establish execution
in this model. A new fleet model would require its own rules/design and ODD review.

Once that producer exists, implement one `/world` vehicle selection through the
normal picker, binding a bounded chase rig to that exact run/vehicle/trip tuple.
Read progress and clock from the same versioned run. Preserve trip identity on
pause and seek; never silently switch to a subsequent trip. Stop at the last
confirmed point during gaps, distinguish before-coverage from after-arrival, and
clear stale pose when changing runs. Missing destinations remain unavailable.
Material risks are false vehicle identity, interpolation across gaps, clock
conversion errors and camera updates continuing after disposal.

Required verification remains:

- Unit/integration tests for successful playback, exact trip retention across
  pause/seek, missing initial coverage, internal gaps, completion, unsupported
  decorative/passenger markers, malformed records and read failures.
- Deterministic replay and seed-matched producer fixtures with preserved source
  identity; assert adapters do not mutate or advance model state.
- Normal-entry Playwright journeys for selection, playback and failure recovery,
  mobile controls, keyboard dismissal and visible/restored focus. Reduced motion
  must offer a static/manual view using the same confirmed records.
- Instrument frame pacing and update cost, bound overlay work and verify camera,
  event listener and render-resource cleanup on close, navigation and run change.
