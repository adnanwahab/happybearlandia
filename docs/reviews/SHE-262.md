## Source acceptance evidence

- `world/render.js`: chooses the active Living World/legacy clock, passes the same
  minute to lighting, updates sun position/intensity, ambient light and background,
  and binds each progressively loaded/streamed façade to its actual building.
  Counts refresh from authoritative snapshots; legacy occupancy is scanned once
  per committed minute rather than once per building per frame.
- `world/render/environment/sim-lighting.js`: authored 06–20 solar arc, dawn/dusk
  palette, nighttime, weekend/Sunday-morning tint and dusk lamp strength. Manual
  Graphics presets remain reversible presentation overrides.
- `world/population/model.js`: `buildingHomeCounts` reads every individual,
  groups exact `homeLocation` IDs and counts House `personAtHome` presence.
  `snapshotPopulation` includes these unweighted counts even at visibleLimit 0.
  No model choices, RNG, scheduling, checkpoint schema or rules version change.
- `world/render/city-renderer.js`: defaults to Simulation clock; supplies each
  façade with its own atHome/residents fraction, instead of global vacancy.
  Missing/unmapped residents stay dark. Office/work closure outside weekday
  08–18 takes precedence, including synthetic homes assigned office anchors.
  Nonresidential buildings otherwise use current model occupancy.
- `world/render/buildings/facade-material.js`: stable per-window shader thresholds
  approximate the supplied fraction; zero occupancy guarantees no lit windows.
  A finite building's lit cell count is not an exact integer ratio guarantee.
- `world/render/environment/sky.js` and `city-details.js`: sky tint, street-lamp
  emission and paired moving decorative headlights follow the lighting state.
  Traffic visibility/quality controls still apply; vehicles remain illustrative.
- `world/render/renderer/canvas-renderer.js`: fills its sky with the shared scene
  background. The fallback intentionally omits shader windows/lamp lighting.

These are synthetic modeled presence counts and authored visual assumptions,
not observed housing occupancy, electricity consumption or astronomical data.
The model's synthetic home assignment may use nonresidential map anchors; no
new geographic identity mapping is invented. Visitors do not count as residents
at their assigned home while away. See `world/population/DATA_FORMATS.md`,
`docs/models/population.md` and `world/render/README.md` for the read contract.
