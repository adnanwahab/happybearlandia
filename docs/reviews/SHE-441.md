The existing `/` gallery had native horizontal scroll, perspective cards,
iframe opt-in interaction, category/jump menus and keyboard controls, but arrows
wrapped and touch scroll had no one-sibling bound. Reuse that surface rather
than introduce a second registry or any simulation experiment relationship.
Here, siblings mean adjacent catalog previews within the selected category.

`tools/preview-swipe.js` owns pointer intent and bounded displacement. A drag
requires 10px horizontal intent; release requires 20% card width clamped to
48–100px, plus horizontal dominance. Feedback is capped at 100px, on only the
pressed card. Release selects at most one neighbor; cancellation, lost capture
and multitouch snap back. No velocity fling. Vertical pan and pinch remain native;
interactive cards and controls are excluded. Drag clicks are suppressed for
500ms; keyboard activation is preserved. No navigation is performed by swipes.

`tools/iframe-gallery.js` clamps selection, announces position, exposes edge
`aria-disabled` while retaining button focus, and preserves arrow/Home/End rail
access. Modified shortcuts and embedded controls retain their keys. Category
changes cancel dragging. OS and saved media reduction remove perspective,
drag transforms and animated scrolling; live reduction cancels an active drag.
`index.html` owns the small CSS additions. No animation libraries/assets added.

No model, snapshot, ID, clock, input, seed, event, provenance or ODD rules changed.
No model outputs are used to drive or substantiate presentation feedback.
The configured `.github/workflows/auto-merge.yml` safety hold (`if: false`)
and `ops/patch-voting/queue.js` default five-upvote threshold remain unchanged,
as do auth and check requirements.
