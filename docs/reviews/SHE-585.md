source IDs, but have no creator authentication, consent or editorial registry.

a public upload queue, a new model, inferred run mappings or fabricated examples.

attestation; editorial review must establish suitability. Legacy shares cannot
be claimed. Lost owner keys have no recovery in this bounded increment.
