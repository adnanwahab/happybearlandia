## Delivered slice and limits

House's Family milestones dialog lazily reads up to eight immutable saves on the
selected branch. Moving, job, relationship and household categories use explicit
record allowlists. Starting-state attributes and between-save differences create
no entries. Each entry exposes its immutable source API link, record path and
recorded payload. Category and exact-tick filters intersect. Exact seek opens only
a saved checkpoint at the recorded event tick; absent snapshots remain explicit.

Relationship records store minutes, so integral tick conversion uses the source
runtime's versioned Population clock. No monthly City identity or time mapping is
implied. Person-only events use membership observed at the source checkpoint;
past membership is not reconstructed. Roommate acceptance means agreement to
physical co-residence, not completed arrival or financial household reassignment.
Mutable autosessions and unrecorded live changes intentionally supply no history.
These limits appear in milestones-contract.md and the dialog.

Implementation paths: world/house/milestones.js, milestones-ui.js,
milestones-contract.md, and app.js (mount and cleanup). No simulation rule, random
draw, clock, ID, seed, retention or write contract changes. ODD inventory explicitly
classifies family history as a view of existing records, so no new model or ODD
rule update is needed. Producer paths checked against presentation:
world/population/contracts.js, behavior.js, economy.js, population-growth.js;
world/house/roommates.js and relationships.js. Discrepancy resolved: an event's
recorded time does not establish an exact snapshot; unavailable seeks are omitted
instead of snapping to the nearest save or recomputing history.
