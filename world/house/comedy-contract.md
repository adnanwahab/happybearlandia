# Gentle household comedy

This update excludes escalation: no escalating arguments, increasingly extravagant choices, forced ceremonies, penalties or automatic interruptions. `Moments` is optional.

`home.systems.comedy` retains up to 16 source-backed highlights, 8 running jokes, 8 paper props, 24 character claims, 12 interviews and 24 command receipts. Only the canonical completed-action hook creates accomplishment highlights. Cancelled work cannot earn them. Ordinary meal, chair use, repairs, compliments and household work support distinct authored reactions; a missing-portion story requires an actual finite economy meal receipt and exhausted portions. These scenes do not change money, hunger, skill, friendship or romance. Existing successful actions keep their normal effects.

Each resident has two stable comic tendencies derived from their actual personality/identity. Highlights record participants and who subsequently heard about the moment. Sharing and callbacks require actual residents present in the same room. Uninformed residents cannot interview or recall an event. Callbacks vary over four appearances, then retire with a one-day cooldown. Character theories are stored separately from the actual event; they cannot overwrite its evidence.

Paper plant hats, official kitchen notes, napping awards, toy meetings and cushion signs are modest cosmetic crafts using actual suitable installed objects. `placeMischief` takes five actual attended minutes. Napping awards require an actual completed rest. `companionCare` takes ten attended minutes and records attention only after completion. No purchases, inventory or food are fabricated. Discoveries require the resident to be in the prop’s room. The renderer anchors props to the saved source object and disposes their resources.

The 3D presentation includes dry looks, inspection glances, proud poses, jacket adjustments, delayed applause and suppressed laughter. Actual personalities choose response styles. Retained highlight playback uses only a cosmetic clock; it never resimulates or awards the source action. Pets have simple game-clock rest/sleep/attention intentions; their cosmetic movement checks floor and furniture clearance and does not claim to be a human navigation action.

Root integration: `mountHouseComedy` is connected in the house app through the normal household-system authority and refresh/autosave. No external service or database migration is required; prior homes acquire the optional system through normal actions.
