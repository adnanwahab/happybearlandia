How to interpret these results

Plans share one seed and are compared to the same no-change baseline at the final month. Mean commute uses completed modeled trip records and can change when the number of trips changes. Proposed costs are catalog inputs. Projects can be delayed, and the future view shows each policy’s modeled stage.

These runs use a synthetic City scenario. They describe outcomes within the selected search space and assumptions.
