# Rashomon Mode

Open `/world/civilization/perspectives/` after recording the seeded demo in the World Time Machine. This read-only first release uses the completed monthly CityState run, the recorded district flood-damage event, the month before the flood, and the exact flood-month checkpoint.

The perspective rules select one resident per household across four situations. A prior occupied unit that is under renovation by month end provides housing exposure evidence. A separate city transport event supplies evidence for the unreachable commute. The out-of-district perspective requires the resident's housing unit to be unchanged. The comparison uses month-end state and makes no claim that the flood caused every difference. Interpretations are editorial examples. Resident beliefs, conversations, and access to news are unavailable in these records.

The seed, city model version, perspective rules version, and source links are visible. Broader scenarios need their own exposure and interpretation rules and a supported recorded run. Population and House Simulator identities use a different clock and are outside this view.
