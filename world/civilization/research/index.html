Inspect the planner’s assumptions

The planner freezes the starting route and trip demand. For each link, it adds 10 minutes for every unit that its demand-to-capacity ratio exceeds one. It assumes the full capacity increase is available. The City model can change routes, lose trips during floods, and take time to deliver the project.
