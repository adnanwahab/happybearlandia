Housing, development and population rules
  • When vacancy is low or more households are searching, the housing system can raise asking rents on available units.
  • Higher rents can make a development proposal more profitable. Construction costs, financing, capacity and utility headroom can delay or prevent new supply.
  • Completed projects add units. The annual household growth rule also responds to housing and job vacancies, so demand can change after supply expands.
