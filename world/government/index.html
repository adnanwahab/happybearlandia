Government process simulator

Application → agency → queue → staff → processing → approval or denial → appeal.

Illustrative DMV assumptions: paper visit 76 minutes and 12 days review; online submission 8 minutes and 2 days review. Queue and staff processing add actual latency. These are scenario inputs, not measured forecasts.

What government sees

Observation policy changes visible information. It does not automatically improve services or change resident outcomes. Future policy choices remain a separate decision.

Request trace

Staff work continuously in this prototype; office calendars, routed trips, calibrated trust, ballots and citizens’ assembly deliberation are unavailable. Costs and missed work are reported, without changing city or household ledgers. One-form reuse requires a prior approved form for the same resident.
