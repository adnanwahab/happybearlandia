      Model assumptions

      The synthetic grid uses local downhill transfer with closed outer edges. Rain adds depth, and drainage removes water up to the outlet’s capacity. Detention lowers the selected terrain cell. A raised road changes its surface elevation. Each step is a model step with an unspecified real duration.

      Road links close at 0.20 m on any sampled road cell. A building is exposed at 0.15 m on any footprint cell. Access uses open links to the modeled depot. Elevations, costs, and rules are illustrative.
