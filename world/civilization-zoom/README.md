# Civilization Zoom

Open `/world/civilization-zoom/` and choose an existing saved Living World. Drill from World into the saved city, district, recorded home anchor, financial household, and resident. The checkpoint ID, simulation tick, and minute remain visible across levels. The URL preserves the full selection. `?experiment=<save>&person=<resident>` resolves the resident’s hierarchy directly from that save.

District and building grouping use each resident’s saved home district and home location. A financial household can span several anchors. The selected anchor shows the members linked to it and the household’s full member count. Residents without building anchors are available in a separate group. The scale diagrams are schematic. The relationship view displays only retained ties and memories, and decisions come from retained trace records.

The World link opens the same save. The House link uses Playable Life to restore that exact resident and checkpoint without resuming a newer household autosave. The family link uses the same Population identity. No association is made with residents in the monthly City engine, Inner Lives, or Worldline.
