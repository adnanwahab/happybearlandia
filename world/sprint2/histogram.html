100-Future Outcome Histogram

How does adding transit capacity change one Riverside worker’s commute across 100 possible futures?

The experiment

Each pair starts from the same synthetic Living City state and shares a seed and any flood shock. One run follows the baseline. The other proposes adding 8 units of Riverside transit capacity at a modeled cost of $160,000.

We compare the worker’s recorded commute time in the final month of each 12-month run. Negative values mean a shorter commute with the proposal.
