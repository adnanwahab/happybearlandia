# Crisis Judgment Drill v1

Normal entry: `/world/challenges.html` → Try the Crisis Judgment Drill → Load
pinned demo → choose one response → Commit decision and inspect. No source is
loaded implicitly. Completed decisions and independent alternative reviews live
only in this tab; repeating verifies the entire receipt without replacing history.
Cancel publishes no partial receipt. Source/network/version failures leave actions
unavailable and explain why. No storage, uploads, live warnings or shared scores.

## Frozen recipe and interpretation

`crisis-demo.v1.json` is the committed authored fixture, generated by
`createDemoDrill()` in `crisis.js`. Validation requires exact canonical equality
with that recipe and the existing challenge SHA256 checks. Unknown model versions,
changed seeds, checkpoints, objectives or outcomes are refused without migration.
Hashes establish consistency, not authorship. Preserve the source revision with
any externally retained receipt. No external observations or provider is required.

Three independent City initial states: 12 households, seeds 42/73/101, month 0;
Riverside/d1 flood starts month 10, lasts 3 months, severity 0.7; horizon month 12.
Private parcel capacity is explicitly zero to suppress unrelated redevelopment.
Reserve adds no intervention; drainage requests resilience +0.4 for 3,000 synthetic
USD; homes requests 2 homes for 4,000 synthetic USD. Existing administration,
funding and construction gates determine activation. These are preparation choices
on a coarse monthly clock, not rescue actions or calibrated engineering costs.

Objectives are frozen before reveal: at month 12 at most one unhoused household;
neither unhoused count nor experienced commute worse than matched reserve within
the registered metric tolerances; both branches within 4,000 public capital USD.
All checks must pass per seed. No Pareto improvement is required; equality passes.
The inherited challenge Pareto verdict in raw receipts is distinct from drill
objective status. Costs exclude private and operating spending. No scalar ethical
score or best policy is inferred. The initial fixture demonstrates a hard limit:
these endpoints show no housing/commute benefit from either project by month 12.
Expenditure differs; failure to see an endpoint benefit does not establish uselessness.
Reserve has 1/1/2 unhoused households on seeds 42/73/101, hence pass/pass/fail.

## Provenance, missing evidence and replay

The briefing is scripted flavor. Initial conditions/costs are authored synthetic
assumptions; all reported outcome numbers are executed City outputs. Measured
observations and uncertainty about real emergency outcomes are unavailable.
Each seed is compared only with its own frozen initial hash, model, month and
reserve branch. Seed variation is not a probability distribution or confidence
interval. Missing metric values stay unavailable, never zero or success.

A completed receipt retains the full scenario, all original household/district
identities, final states for both branches, metric sources/units, monthly public
capital ledgers and grades. Initial and final states are deeply frozen; no
intermediate checkpoint is invented. Alternative reviews recompute from the same
scenario rather than mutate a completed decision. Replay compares every receipt
field, not merely seed or grade, and rejects discrepancies. Cancellation and
execution failures return no completed receipt.

## Model boundary and verification

This orchestrates unchanged Monthly City (`engine.js`, `systems.js`, `seed.js`,
`contracts.js`) through `challenges/contract.js`; no new behavioral rules, RNG,
resident identities, social model or clock. Compare the [City ODD](../../docs/models/city.md)
and [City equations](../city/README.md) with those paths before approval.

Run `bun test test/crisis-drill.test.js test/challenge-contract.test.js` and
`bunx playwright test --config test/crisis-drill.browser.config.js`. Set
`HBL_BUN_PATH` to the real Bun executable if a local package shim shadows it.
No repository-wide lint/typecheck script exists; these are plain JavaScript
modules and this slice introduces no TypeScript interface.
